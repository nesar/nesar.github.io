Galaxy Cluster Cosmology and Surveys

The SPTpol Extended Cluster Survey exemplifies leading research in galaxy cluster cosmology, employing millimeter-wave observations to detect galaxy clusters via the Sunyaev-Zel'dovich (SZ) effect. This method provides a mass-selected sample, largely independent of cluster redshift, crucial for cosmological analysis. Key methods include developing advanced SZ detection pipelines, careful characterization of survey selection functions, and robust calibration of the cluster mass-observable relation, often through multi-wavelength follow-up. The impact is profound: these surveys yield large, statistically powerful cluster samples, enabling precise constraints on fundamental cosmological parameters like the dark energy equation of state, neutrino mass, and the amplitude of matter fluctuations (sigma8), thus probing cosmic structure growth and potentially resolving cosmological tensions.
