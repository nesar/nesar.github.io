Cosmic Web & Structure Formation

My research focuses on understanding the fine-grained structure of the Cosmic Web and dark matter haloes through a multistream perspective. I develop methods to map the topology and geometry of this intricate web, revealing its fundamental characteristics. My work investigates how dark matter evolves from a smooth initial state into caustics, filaments, and halos. We employ a multistream view to unravel the detailed formation of structures, providing a more complete portrait of the universe's large-scale architecture. This approach allows us to precisely characterize the dark matter web, offering critical insights into structure formation processes and their observable signatures.

Machine Learning for Astrophysics & Cosmology

My research focuses on leveraging advanced machine learning to address grand challenges in astrophysics and cosmology. I develop deep learning pipelines for tasks like strong gravitational lens detection and modeling, and employ generative adversarial networks for robust anomaly detection in astronomical images. Our work also involves building foundation models for cosmological simulations and training LLMs for spectroscopic analysis. I create emulators for complex phenomena like the matter power spectrum in modified gravity, and develop differentiable models for large-scale structure. Through these methods, we aim to improve galaxy selection for weak lensing, estimate peculiar velocities, and achieve probabilistic redshift estimation, all while physically benchmarking AI-evolved cosmological structure formation.

General Machine Learning & Scientific Computing

My research focuses on developing advanced machine learning for scientific computing, emphasizing probabilistic modeling and deep learning. I create robust reduced-order surrogates for complex systems, like fluid flows, explicitly quantifying uncertainty. My work also addresses global field reconstruction from sparse sensor data using techniques like Voronoi tessellation. We enhance interpretability in generative models by achieving statistically disentangled latent spaces guided by factors in scientific datasets. This integrated approach leverages probabilistic neural networks, Gaussian processes, and automated machine learning to provide efficient, interpretable, and uncertainty-aware solutions for high-dimensional scientific challenges.

Galactic Structure & Stellar Populations

My research focuses on mapping the three-dimensional structure of the Milky Way using a vast sample of 2.6 million Red Clump stars. I develop methods to precisely identify these stars photometrically and leverage their reliability as standard candles to determine accurate distances across the galaxy, from its inner regions to the far outer disk. This extensive dataset allows me to trace the Galactic bar, spiral arm features, and the warp of the disk with unprecedented detail. My work provides a crucial empirical foundation for understanding our galaxy's morphology, kinematics, and the distribution of its stellar populations, offering new insights into its formation and evolution.
