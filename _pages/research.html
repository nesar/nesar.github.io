Cosmic Structure & Dark Matter Dynamics

My research focuses on unraveling the intricate dynamics of cosmic structure formation. I investigate the fundamental role of dark matter streams, mapping their multistream caustic network that defines the topology and geometry of the Cosmic Web and dark matter haloes. We develop sophisticated AI and machine learning techniques, including differentiable neural networks, to predict large-scale structure with high fidelity. My work also involves robustly benchmarking these AI-generated structures against physical principles. Furthermore, I create efficient emulators for the matter power spectrum, enabling precise exploration of modified gravity cosmologies. This comprehensive approach advances our understanding of dark matter and the universe's architectural blueprint.

AI/ML for Observational Astrophysics

My research focuses on applying AI/ML techniques to address critical challenges in observational astrophysics. I develop methods for precise parameter estimation, using machine learning for probabilistic redshift estimation from synthetic spectra and deep neural networks for peculiar velocity estimation. My work includes building modular deep learning pipelines for detecting and modeling galaxy-scale strong gravitational lenses, crucial for understanding dark matter. I also employ generative adversarial networks for robust anomaly detection in vast galaxy image datasets, enabling the discovery of peculiar astronomical objects. We optimize galaxy selection for accurate weak lensing cluster mass estimation and process millions of red clump stars to map the Milky Way, significantly enhancing our understanding of cosmic structure and evolution.

Advanced AI/ML Methodologies for Science

My research focuses on advancing AI/ML methodologies to accelerate scientific discovery. I develop multi-modal foundation models for complex scientific data, such as cosmological simulations, to enable holistic analysis and feature extraction. My work also benchmarks AI-evolved cosmological structure formation, aiming for faster and more accurate simulations than traditional methods. Scientific Machine Learning for Complex Physics

My research focuses on developing Scientific Machine Learning (SciML) methodologies for complex physics. I develop probabilistic neural networks and reduced-order models to create efficient surrogates for systems like high-dimensional stress fields and fluid flows. My work integrates methods such as Gaussian process emulation and Voronoi tessellation-assisted deep learning for robust data recovery and global field reconstruction, even from sparse sensor data. Through these approaches, I aim to provide computationally efficient, non-intrusive models that quantify uncertainty, enabling accurate predictions and deeper understanding of intricate physical phenomena.
