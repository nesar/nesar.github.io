My research focuses on developing and applying computational methods at the intersection of astrophysics, cosmology, and machine learning. Below are my main research areas with representative figures from recent publications.

Foundation Models

Developing large language models and foundation models specialized for astronomy, including domain-specific LLMs for scientific research and education.

Machine Learning for Science

Applying machine learning techniques to astronomical problems, including neural networks for data analysis, generative models, and anomaly detection in scientific datasets.

Dark Matter & Cosmology

Investigating the cosmic web structure, dark matter halos, and large-scale structure formation using N-body simulations and multi-stream analysis.

Representative figures coming soon...

Emulation & Inference

Developing statistical emulators, surrogate models, and uncertainty quantification methods for cosmological simulations and scientific inference.
