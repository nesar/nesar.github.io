Cosmic Structure & Dynamics

My work investigates the intricate fine-grained structure of the cosmic web and dark matter haloes. I develop a novel multi-stream view, recognizing dark matter as distinct, overlapping flows rather than a smooth fluid. This approach allows us to precisely map the topology and geometry of the dark matter web, identifying crucial features like caustics – phase-space singularities where streams accumulate. Understanding these multi-stream dynamics is essential for a complete "portrait" of cosmic structure. Our findings illuminate the fundamental design of the universe's scaffolding, providing a more detailed view that holds vital implications for future direct and indirect dark matter detection experiments.

Machine Learning for Cosmology

My research focuses on advancing machine learning for cosmology. I develop multi-modal foundation models to extract insights from complex cosmological simulation data and create differentiable neural networks like SHAMNet for precise large-scale structure predictions. My work also involves building efficient emulators, such as for the matter power spectrum in f(R) modified gravity, to accelerate parameter inference. Furthermore, I apply deep learning to observational data, like estimating peculiar velocities from the kinetic Sunyaev-Zel'dovich effect. A critical aspect of my research is physically benchmarking AI-generated cosmic web and structure formation, ensuring our models robustly capture the universe's underlying physics and enhance our understanding of cosmic evolution.

Machine Learning for Astronomical Observations

My research focuses on leveraging machine learning to advance astronomical observation analysis. I develop methods for interpreting complex data, such as teaching large language models to understand spectroscopy, thereby enhancing our ability to extract scientific insights. My work also includes generating synthetic spectra with machine learning for robust probabilistic redshift estimation, crucial for cosmological studies. Furthermore, I implement generative adversarial networks (GANs) for efficient anomaly detection in diverse astronomical images, from Hyper Suprime-Cam galaxy observations to broader datasets, uncovering unexpected phenomena. We also build modular deep learning pipelines to automate intricate tasks like detecting and modeling galaxy-scale strong gravitational lenses, significantly accelerating discovery in the era of large-scale surveys.

Advanced Data Science & Scientific Computation

My research focuses on developing cutting-edge data science and scientific computation methodologies. I advance probabilistic machine learning and deep learning, particularly utilizing probabilistic neural networks and Gaussian process emulation to build robust reduced-order models and surrogates for complex systems, like fluid flows. My work enhances interpretability by disentangling latent spaces in generative models and optimizes data selection to reduce error, notably in astrophysical weak lensing. I also address high-dimensional data and global field reconstruction from sparse sensors using techniques like Voronoi tessellation-assisted deep learning. Our methods are crucial for efficient large-scale data analysis, from stellar populations to accurate stress field predictions, significantly improving scientific understanding and prediction.
