Foundation Models

Foundation model research is transforming scientific discovery, especially in astrophysics and cosmology. Core methodologies involve domain-specializing large language models (LLMs) and developing multi-modal architectures for complex scientific data, including cosmological simulations and spectroscopy. These specialized models, ranging from efficient 8B to powerful 70B parameters, achieve benchmark-topping performance in astronomy Q&A, often matching or exceeding general-purpose models like GPT-4o. This work extends to creating "smart assistants" and "agentic AI" systems that aid data interpretation and fundamental physics reasoning. Establishing rigorous evaluation frameworks is crucial, affirming these models' substantial impact as scientific research assistants, accelerating inquiry across diverse domains.

Machine Learning for Science

Machine learning (ML) transforms astronomy, addressing complex data challenges. Neural networks enhance image quality via point spread function deconvolution. Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs) detect anomalies in galaxy images, uncovering unusual phenomena. Deep learning pipelines automate strong gravitational lens detection and modeling, accelerating cosmological studies. Unsupervised ML redefines galaxy morphology and identifies rare stellar populations. Research emphasizes enhancing interpretability using disentangled latent spaces, ensuring scientific rigor. These advancements underscore ML's indispensable role in extracting unprecedented insights from astronomical data.

Dark Matter & Cosmology

This research advances Dark Matter & Cosmology through sophisticated methods. Cosmological simulations, including AI-evolved models like SHAMNet and CRK-HACC, are crucial for tracing large-scale structure and galaxy formation, with rigorous physical benchmarking ensuring accuracy. Multi-stream analyses reveal the intricate topology and caustic structure of dark matter halos and the cosmic web. Observational efforts, exemplified by the SPTpol cluster survey and Hyper Suprime-Cam cosmic shear, provide data to constrain cosmological parameters and alternative gravity theories like f(R) gravity, and to validate simulations. Future missions such as SPHEREx promise further high-precision insights, collectively refining our understanding of the Universe’s evolution and composition.

Emulation & Inference

Emulation and inference research harnesses advanced machine learning, notably probabilistic neural networks and deep learning, to construct efficient surrogates and accelerate scientific discovery. These methods address high-dimensional problems across fields like fluid dynamics, astrophysics, and materials science, enabling rapid global field reconstruction, precise parameter estimation (e.g., redshift, peculiar velocities), and complex system emulation (e.g., matter power spectra, fluid flows). A crucial focus is on interpretable uncertainty quantification, ensuring robust and reliable predictions from sparse or incomplete data. This approach significantly enhances model efficiency, accuracy, and trustworthiness for complex physical phenomena.
