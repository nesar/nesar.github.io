The intersection of advanced artificial intelligence and machine learning with scientific research has rapidly become a frontier of innovation, offering powerful tools to unravel complex phenomena across disciplines. From astrophysics and cosmology to materials science and quantum chemistry, scientists are grappling with increasingly vast and intricate datasets generated by simulations, telescopes, and experimental facilities. Traditional analytical methods often struggle to extract meaningful insights from such high-dimensional, multi-modal information, prompting a critical need for sophisticated computational approaches that can accelerate discovery, enhance predictive capabilities, and facilitate deeper scientific understanding.

This domain focuses on leveraging state-of-the-art AI/ML methodologies to address fundamental scientific questions. Key areas of investigation include developing robust models that can interpret and generate scientific data, designing algorithms that learn physical laws from observations, and creating frameworks that ensure the interpretability and trustworthiness of AI-driven insights. These efforts are crucial for tasks such as accelerating computationally expensive simulations, identifying subtle patterns in complex datasets, guiding experimental design, and automating data analysis, thereby fundamentally changing the paradigm of scientific exploration.

My work directly contributes to this burgeoning field by developing and applying advanced AI/ML methodologies to complex scientific challenges, particularly in cosmology and materials science. I have focused on creating foundational AI models capable of handling diverse scientific data, as exemplified by my development of a Multi-modal Foundation Model for Cosmological Simulation Data. This model integrates disparate data types from cosmological simulations to learn underlying physical relationships, offering unprecedented predictive power and efficiency in understanding cosmic evolution. Furthermore, I have explored the frontier of AI-driven algorithm design, establishing rigorous benchmarks for AI-evolved cosmological structure formation algorithms, demonstrating their potential to accelerate simulations while maintaining scientific accuracy.

In addition to simulation and data integration, I have pioneered novel applications of Large Language Models (LLMs) and advanced generative AI for scientific discovery. My research includes "Teaching LLMs to Speak Spectroscopy," where I adapt and fine-tune LLMs to understand, interpret, and generate insights from complex spectroscopic data, offering new avenues for automated material characterization and astronomical data analysis. Crucially, I have also prioritized model transparency and reliability through my work on "Enhancing Interpretability in Generative Modeling: Statistically Disentangled Latent Spaces Guided by Generative Factors in Scientific Datasets." This research ensures that generative models produce scientifically meaningful outputs by explicitly mapping their latent variables to physical parameters, fostering trust and enabling hypothesis generation directly from the model's internal representations.
